# Learn Office Energy with Phoebe

Desk Energy Reset: six 45-minute sessions on winning back energy at a desk with small movement
breaks. What long sitting does and does not do, why there is no single correct posture, a
three-minute routine with stick-figure guides, a break bench, eyes and wrists at the screen, and
making the break automatic.

- `index.html` - landing page, session cards, knowledge map
- `courses/01-...06-` - the six sessions; session 4 is the bench
- `assets/oe-live.js` - the break bench and routine timer (runs in the browser, modelled)
- `materials/oe_reference.py` - independent Python reference; `node materials/oe-node.js` and
  `python3 materials/oe_reference.py --json` print identical JSON

The running case, Priyanka, is constructed. Not medical advice.

By Phoebe Fu.
