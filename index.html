The six sessions 🌿 · from the evidence to a break that happens by itself

The running case is Priyanka, a constructed analyst at an invented logistics firm. She works 08:30 to 17:30, eats lunch at her keyboard, and by mid-afternoon feels flat and stiff. Priyanka, her firm and every number about her day are invented for teaching, and every page says so.

Not medical advice. These pages explain research on sitting and movement at work. They do not diagnose or treat anything. If you have pain that is getting worse, chest pain, numbness, tingling or any symptom that worries you, see a doctor or physiotherapist before trying the moves. Session 6 lists the warning signs.

What is honest here 🧾

Desk wellbeing advice is full of famous lines that are overstated or have no source. Each claim on these pages carries its status.

ClaimStatus
Every bench figure: longest sit, bouts over 30 minutes, standing and moving minutes, dropped breaksModelled in your browser, one minute at a time, from the schedule on the widget; Priyanka's day is constructed and yours never leaves the page
"Sitting is the new smoking"Overstated. Sitting carries a real risk; a 2018 commentary puts it at under a tenth of heavy smoking's absolute toll. Session 1
"There is one correct posture"Contested. Physiotherapy researchers say the evidence is weak; the NHS still advises sitting upright. Both agree staying still is the problem. Session 2
"10,000 steps a day"No special basis for that number. Two large studies find benefits level off lower, depending on age. Session 6
